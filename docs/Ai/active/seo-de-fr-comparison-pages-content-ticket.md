## Product/content principles

Follow Mindrink public-copy style:

- user-centric, calm, concrete;
- no exaggerated claims;
- no unexplained comparatives like “better” or “clearer” unless the sentence explains why;
- no guilt/shame language;
- natural localized copy with correct accents/umlauts;
- mention Mindrink’s differentiators honestly: simple, private, lightweight tracking, useful reflection/reporting, not a medical service.

Do not keyword-stuff. Use query language only where it reads naturally.

## Scope

### German page changes

Modify:

`de/blog/best-alcohol-tracking-apps.html`

Recommended content changes:

1. Update the `<title>` to more directly match query language.

Suggested direction:

`Beste Apps zum Tracken von Alkoholkonsum 2026 | Mindrink`

If that is too long for the current template, use:

`Beste Apps zum Alkoholkonsum-Tracking 2026 | Mindrink`

2. Update the meta description to keep the same promise but use natural German query language.

Suggested direction:

`Welche App hilft dir, deinen Alkoholkonsum zu tracken? Vergleiche einfache Tracker, Reduktionsprogramme und Challenge-Apps – aktualisiert für 2026.`

Only include `aktualisiert für 2026` if the implementer actually reviews and refreshes the page content for 2026.

3. Update the H1 to make the page intent immediately clear.

Suggested direction:

`Beste Apps zum Tracken von Alkoholkonsum: Welche passt zu dir?`

4. Add or revise the opening paragraph so it answers the search intent before listing apps.

Suggested direction:

Explain that people may be looking for different kinds of alcohol apps:

- simple logging/tracking;
- controlled drinking / reduction support;
- challenges or abstinence periods;
- deeper programs/coaching.

Then say the page compares those approaches so readers can choose based on their goal.

5. Add a short section for controlled drinking intent.

Suggested H2:

`Welche App passt zu kontrolliertem Trinken?`

Content direction:

- explain that controlled drinking users often need quick logging, trend visibility, and reflection rather than pressure;
- differentiate neutral tracking from abstinence/challenge apps and structured reduction programs;
- keep this educational, not salesy.

6. Make the Reframe mention useful for `reframe app deutsch` intent without making unverified claims.

Content direction:

- If the page already mentions Reframe, add one sentence that users searching for Reframe in German should check language support, program style, and whether the app’s goal fits their own.
- Do not claim Reframe is or is not available in German unless verified from a current source.

7. Add natural internal links to related German pages if they exist.

Potential anchor directions:

- `Alkoholkonsum tracken`
- `kontrollierter trinken`
- `Alkoholeinheiten verstehen`
- `Alkohol-Tracking im Alltag`

Only link to pages that exist and are relevant. Do not create broken links.

## Non-goals

Do not:

- rewrite unrelated pages;
- change the calculator page;
- change the methodology page;
- add fake rankings or unsupported “best overall” claims;
- add a top-10 format unless the page genuinely compares 10 apps;
- make medical claims;
- make unverified claims about competitors;
- change canonical URLs or hreflang structure unless a concrete broken tag is found;
- introduce tracking/analytics;
- create new pages.
