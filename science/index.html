Science at Mindrink

Science is part of how Mindrink works

Mindrink is not a tracker built around arbitrary colours or random limits chosen to make the numbers feel reassuring. We use science and research to guide what we calculate, how we present it and what we say—and we show the sources behind those choices.

Our goal is to ground every important choice in the best available evidence, and to be clear when evidence is incomplete.

An evidence-led history series

The Origins of Alcohol

Alcohol is deeply embedded in human civilization. It brings pleasure, connects people and shapes traditions, yet it can also alter judgment and cause profound harm. How did such a powerful substance become so central to our cultures, rituals and everyday lives?

The Origins of Alcohol traces humanity's relationship with alcohol, from its earliest archaeological evidence to the ways societies embraced its effects and confronted its dangers. Through ancient texts, archaeology and scientific research, we explore how that relationship took shape.
