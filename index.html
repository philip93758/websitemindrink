Before you download

Is Mindrink free?
Yes. There is no subscription and no advertising.
Do I need an account?
No. You can start tracking without registering.
Do I have to be trying to quit?
No. You can simply keep a record and understand your patterns.
Where are my drinking records stored?
Your drinking records stay on your device. Analytics events do not include the drink type or amount you enter. Usage analytics are enabled by default and use an app-generated ID, not your name. On iOS, they may include recordings of screen interactions with standard text and input fields masked. You can turn analytics off in Settings. Technical error reports are collected separately and are not disabled by that switch.Privacy details
